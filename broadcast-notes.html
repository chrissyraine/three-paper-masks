Synopsis

Three Paper Masks is a broadcast in fragments. Each chapter recovered, dated approximately, redacted where necessary. A mother. Two boys. Animal masks made of paper — a horse, a pig, a llama. A rabbit, never worn, always present. A trail that does not end cleanly. A story that does not explain itself.

The series arrives weekly. What it means is left to the viewer. What it documents is not confirmed.
